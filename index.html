Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our products and services

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ) for bulk orders? ▼
Our MOQ varies by product. For most pharmaceutical products, we require a minimum of 10 boxes per SKU. For hospitals and large institutions, we offer flexible terms. Contact us for specific product MOQ details.
Do you offer delivery across the Philippines? ▼
Yes, we deliver nationwide! We have logistics partners covering Luzon, Visayas, and Mindanao. Delivery times range from 1-3 days for Metro Manila and 3-7 days for provincial areas.
Are your products FDA approved? ▼
Absolutely. All our pharmaceutical products are registered with the Philippine Food and Drug Administration (FDA). We maintain strict quality control and only source from licensed manufacturers.
How can I request a price list or quotation? ▼
Simply contact us via WhatsApp, email, or give us a call. Our sales team will provide you with a detailed quotation based on your requirements and volume.
Do you offer discounts for regular bulk buyers? ▼
Yes, we offer tiered pricing and volume discounts for our regular B2B partners. Contact us to discuss a customized pricing structure for your business.
What payment methods do you accept? ▼
We accept bank transfers, GCash, and other major payment methods. For regular B2B partners, we also offer net payment terms upon approval.
